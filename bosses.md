# Emberdeep Boss Development Plan

This document describes the implementation plan for the ten campaign bosses from
Section 10 of the Emberdeep game specification. It also defines how gear and
player progression influence each fight without allowing stats to replace skill.

No boss implementation is included here. This is the design and sequencing
document to follow before gameplay code is changed.

## Design goals

- Every boss remains a short skill mini-game lasting approximately 30–90 seconds.
- Player input, timing, positioning, memory, and pattern recognition remain the
  primary success factors.
- Gear must matter enough that upgrading equipment improves consistency and
  reduces the punishment for mistakes.
- Strong gear must not allow a player to ignore the boss mechanic completely.
- All combat calculations use deterministic integer or basis-point math.
- Random spawns, sequences, attacks, and pattern choices use the shared PCG32 RNG.
- Boss data, tuning values, charts, and attack scripts remain data-driven.
- The same rules must be usable by the graphical client and testable in `core`.

## Shared campaign model

### Fight lifecycle

Every boss uses the same high-level lifecycle:

1. Locked, available, or cleared campaign state.
2. Procedural title card with the boss silhouette, location, description,
   recommended level, recommended gear tier, and rewards.
3. Ready action.
4. 3-2-1 countdown.
5. Active mini-game.
6. Pause and Give Up controls.
7. Result screen with score, accuracy, hearts lost, stars, and rewards.
8. Save only completed rewards and best-star improvements.

### Hearts and defensive scaling

Each fight starts with:

`startingHearts = 3 + min(Ward, 2)`

Ward is capped at two extra hearts. Gear therefore improves survivability, but
it cannot create an unlimited mistake buffer.

When a mechanic causes a hit or mistake, it removes one heart unless that
mechanic explicitly uses another failure resource, such as Morwenna's Groove
gauge. Reaching zero hearts immediately defeats the fight.

### Offensive scaling

Bosses have a `bossHealth` or `bossStability` value representing how much
successful performance is required to defeat them. The value is not displayed
as a conventional RPG health bar in every mini-game; it is translated into the
appropriate mechanic:

- Burst bosses require more effective damage during a time window.
- Timing bosses require more successful hits or deal less counter-damage per
  success.
- Rhythm bosses require a higher effective Groove target or more sustained
  accuracy.
- Target bosses require more real targets.
- Dodge bosses require survival plus periodic damage opportunities.
- Gauntlet bosses use phase-specific health requirements.

The base difficulty is determined by boss index and recommended gear tier.
Player gear modifies the effective requirement within a bounded range:

`gearPower = clamp(derivedCombatPower / recommendedPower, 0.70, 1.30)`

The player receives a benefit when `gearPower > 1.0` and a penalty when it is
below 1.0, but no gear value can reduce a boss to zero effort.

### Gear contribution

The shared player stats used by campaign fights are:

- **Attack:** increases damage or progress generated by successful actions.
- **Focus:** widens timing windows and reduces score thresholds, capped at 25%.
- **Ward:** grants up to two additional hearts.
- **Speed:** affects movement responsiveness and recovery in movement challenges.
- **Crit chance/damage:** gives bounded bonus progress on successful offensive
  actions where the mechanic allows an attack or counter.
- **Pierce:** reduces defensive or stability mitigation on armored bosses.
- **Max HP/Defense:** reduces the consequence of unavoidable or confirmed hits
  only where the boss mechanic defines damage mitigation.

Each boss should expose a readable breakdown before the fight:

`Skill requirement + gear contribution + current bonuses`

The UI must not hide the reason a high-tier player still needs to perform the
mechanic correctly.

### Suggested scaling formula

For a successful action with a base progress value:

`effectiveProgress = floor(baseProgress × attackMultiplier × defenseMultiplier)`

Where:

- `attackMultiplier = 1 + min(0.80, attack / bossDefense)`
- `defenseMultiplier = 1 + min(0.20, pierce / bossDefense)`
- `bossDefense` is defined per boss and scales by campaign tier.

The exact coefficients are tuning values and belong in boss data. A successful
action must always have a meaningful minimum effect, while a failed action must
never be converted into progress merely because gear is strong.

### Focus integration

Focus follows the specification:

- Timing windows multiply by `1 + focus`.
- Score thresholds divide by `1 + focus`.
- Focus is capped at 25%.

Focus affects precision requirements, not the validity of an input outside the
window. It should never remove a mechanic's input or auto-complete a phase.

### Stars and gear

Stars continue to measure execution quality:

- 1★: complete the boss.
- 2★: meet the boss-specific good-performance target.
- 3★: meet the flawless or near-flawless target.

Gear can make the progress target easier to reach, but it cannot waive
no-miss, no-heart-loss, or accuracy conditions for 3★ unless the specification
explicitly says the target is numeric.

## Stage 1 — Timing and burst foundations

### Boss 1: Grubnok, the Burrow-King

**Mechanic:** Frenzy Dig. Tap the throne rock for 10 seconds. A golden nugget
grants five instant taps.

**Skill implementation:**

- Count mouse clicks and Space presses during a fixed 10-second timer.
- Display current CPS, average CPS, Rage, and remaining time.
- Spawn the nugget with deterministic timing and position.
- Grant the nugget bonus only when the nugget itself is hit.
- End the challenge exactly at the timer boundary.

**Gear scaling:**

- Give each valid tap a base damage value derived from Attack and the equipped
  pickaxe tier.
- Apply a boss `stability` target that increases with the recommended gear tier.
- Convert the final damage to the displayed CPS-equivalent score so the player
  still needs to tap actively.
- Cap gear's effective contribution so a slow player cannot clear through stats.
- Use Focus-adjusted CPS targets:
  - 1★: `5 / (1 + Focus)`.
  - 2★: `7 / (1 + Focus)`.
  - 3★: `9 / (1 + Focus)`.
- Let high Attack increase Rage gained per valid tap, but do not let it generate
  taps automatically.
- Let Crit create an occasional bounded bonus to Rage, not an extra input.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Deterministic nugget spawn tests.
- CPS and timer-boundary tests.
- Attack scaling tests proving gear improves progress but cannot clear with zero
  taps.

### Boss 2: Sir Rattlebone

**Mechanic:** Bone Duel. Stop a moving needle inside a zone for eight rounds.
The zone shrinks from 30% to 8%; misses cost hearts.

**Skill implementation:**

- Use a reusable timing bar with needle position, direction, speed, success zone,
  center zone, and round number.
- Increase needle speed and shrink the success zone each round.
- Accept click or Space to stop the needle.
- Track misses and perfect hits.
- Complete after all eight rounds.

**Gear scaling:**

- Treat each successful stop as a counter attack against Rattlebone's armor.
- Use Attack and Pierce to increase counter damage.
- Define `bossArmor` by round; later rounds have higher mitigation.
- Gear cannot turn a miss into a hit.
- Focus widens the effective timing window; gear does not widen it directly.
- If the player succeeds at every round but has insufficient gear, the fight
  remains a clear but lower-quality result only if the total counter damage
  reaches the boss's stability target.
- A 3★ result still requires no misses and at least four perfect hits.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Zone and needle progression tests.
- Counter-damage tests for Attack, Pierce, and armor.
- Regression test proving misses always cost a heart regardless of gear.

## Stage 2 — Rhythm and target-grid foundations

### Boss 3: Morwenna the Ashen Witch

**Mechanic:** Cinder Dance. Four-lane rhythm challenge, approximately 75
seconds and 110 notes. Groove starts at 50, gains two per hit, loses six per
miss, and reaching zero fails.

**Skill implementation:**

- Load a deterministic chart of note timestamps and lane indices.
- Render four lanes and a hit line.
- Support D/F/J/K and mouse/touch lane input.
- Judge Perfect, Good, and Miss using the specified timing windows.
- Apply Focus to timing windows.
- Stop the fight if Groove reaches zero.
- Calculate accuracy as `(perfect + 0.6 × good) / total`.

**Gear scaling:**

- Add a `witchWard` or `grooveDrainResistance` value derived from defensive
  gear, capped so it never prevents failure.
- On a successful hit, Attack increases a hidden Cinder damage value.
- Morwenna's boss stability increases by tier; damage determines whether the
  player receives the full clear reward after sustaining the required rhythm.
- A miss always costs six Groove; gear may reduce the amount by a small bounded
  percentage, but the minimum loss remains significant.
- Gear cannot create a hit, repair a missed note, or make a zero-accuracy run
  valid.
- Accuracy thresholds remain the star gate:
  - 1★: 70%.
  - 2★: 85%.
  - 3★: 95%.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Chart playback and note judgment tests.
- Groove drain and minimum-drain tests.
- Damage/stability tests across gear tiers.

### Boss 4: Thessaly, the Bog Hydra

**Mechanic:** Head Hunt. A 3×3 grid contains green real heads and blue decoys.
Real heads remain visible for 1.4 to 0.8 seconds; decoys appear 30% of the time.

**Skill implementation:**

- Schedule real heads and decoys with PCG32.
- Support mouse/touch cells and keyboard keys 1–9.
- Track real hits, decoy hits, missed real heads, and time.
- Decoy taps remove a heart; missed real heads have no penalty.
- Run for 35 seconds.
- Clear at 30 real hits and award 3★ at 45 hits with no heart lost.

**Gear scaling:**

- Give each real-head hit a damage value based on Attack and the equipped gear.
- Increase Hydra health per head at higher boss tiers.
- A real hit can therefore count as partial progress when gear is weak and
  stronger progress when gear is appropriate, but the player still needs at
  least the required number of correct target selections.
- Speed gear can add a small grace period to the visible lifetime, capped so the
  player must still identify targets.
- Defensive gear may reduce a decoy penalty only through an explicit limited
  shield charge; it may not make decoys safe indefinitely.
- Decoys never grant progress, regardless of gear.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Seeded grid schedule tests.
- Input-to-cell mapping tests.
- Decoy punishment tests.
- Gear scaling tests proving target identification remains mandatory.

## Stage 3 — Directional reaction and memory

### Boss 5: Aldric, the Gilded Knight

**Mechanic:** Parry Duel. Twenty-four directional attacks. Windows shrink from
600 ms to 350 ms. From attack 12 onward, some telegraphs are feints.

**Skill implementation:**

- Generate a deterministic sequence of Left, Right, and Up attacks.
- Mark later attacks as real or feint.
- Display telegraphs and sword poses.
- Accept arrow keys, swipe directions, and directional touch zones.
- Matching inputs parry real attacks.
- Wrong, late, missing, or feint responses remove a heart.
- Every sixth successful parry opens a Space/tap Counter window.

**Gear scaling:**

- Use Attack and Pierce to determine counter damage against Aldric's armor.
- Set `bossArmor` high enough that low-tier gear can survive only with strong
  execution and high-tier gear can finish more comfortably.
- Speed does not expand the reaction window; Focus does.
- Defensive gear may grant Ward but cannot prevent a failed parry from being
  counted as a failed parry.
- Counter windows remain input-based. Gear increases damage from a successful
  counter, not the number of counters available.
- Clear requires surviving all 24 attacks and defeating the stability target.
- 3★ still requires zero hearts lost.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Direction and feint sequence determinism.
- Reaction-window boundary tests.
- Armor, Pierce, and Counter damage tests.
- Regression test proving a wrong direction always loses a heart.

### Boss 6: Isolde, Runewarden

**Mechanic:** Rune Recall. Repeat ten sequences of six rune types, with length
growing from 3 to 12 and display speed increasing from 600 ms to 350 ms.

**Skill implementation:**

- Define six rune identifiers with stable shape/color presentation.
- Generate a seeded sequence for each round.
- Show the sequence, then enter input mode.
- Support rune taps and keys 1–6.
- On an incorrect rune, remove one heart and replay the round.
- Clear after all ten rounds.
- Award 3★ only with no mistakes.

**Gear scaling:**

- Use Focus to extend rune display time and input tolerance only within the
  global Focus cap.
- Use Intelligence-equivalent stat contributions from the existing Focus/gear
  model to grant a small maximum-sequence assist, never automatic answers.
- Defensive gear affects only hearts.
- A boss memory `stability` value can require the player to complete every round;
  gear does not skip rounds or convert wrong input to correct input.
- Attack can determine the reward-quality or final damage score after a successful
  sequence, but not the validity of the sequence itself.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Seeded sequence tests.
- Round replay tests after mistakes.
- Display timing and Focus tests.
- Regression test proving an incorrect rune always costs a heart.

## Stage 4 — Spatial movement and lane survival

### Boss 7: Seraphel, the Fallen Star

**Mechanic:** Star Trace. Tap numbered stars in order across deterministic
constellations. Early constellations contain 4–5 stars; the fourth and later
constellations contain 5–6 stars. Meteors are hazards.

**Skill implementation:**

- Generate safe, deterministic star layouts.
- Display numbers and a two-second fade timer.
- Require ordered taps.
- Spawn moving meteors.
- Meteor taps cost a heart.
- Run for 40 seconds.
- Clear at eight completed constellations.
- Award 3★ at 12 constellations with no hearts lost.

**Gear scaling:**

- Use Speed to slightly increase star fade time or reduce the fade penalty,
  capped by a strict maximum.
- Use Attack to determine progress per completed constellation against Seraphel's
  stability, while still requiring ordered star completion.
- Use Pierce to reduce the stability requirement slightly at higher gear tiers.
- Defensive gear only affects Ward and cannot make meteor mistakes harmless.
- A player who cannot trace a constellation correctly gains no progress from gear.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Ordered-target validation tests.
- Star fade and meteor collision tests.
- Gear grace-period caps.
- Stability tests proving correct spatial actions remain required.

### Boss 8: Ignathia, the Ember Wyrm

**Mechanic:** Wyrmfire Run. Survive a 45-second three-lane route, collect embers,
and complete 25-tap Scale Break phases every 15 seconds. Lane movement wraps
around, so moving right from the rightmost lane enters the leftmost lane.

**Skill implementation:**

- Move through three lanes with wrapped left/right movement.
- Generate fire waves, ember pickups, and Scale Break checkpoints.
- Support arrows, A/D, swipes, and left/right touch zones.
- Fireball collisions are checked in a defined player-distance band near the
  bottom of the route; resolved projectiles remain absent during a respawn
  cooldown so hazards cannot overlap immediately.
- Scale Break pauses movement and requires 25 taps in three seconds.
- Clear at route completion.
- Award 3★ with at most one heart lost and at least 20 embers.

**Gear scaling:**

- Use Speed to increase lane-switch responsiveness and reduce movement
  interpolation delay, not to teleport the player past hazards.
- Use Attack to increase damage dealt during successful Scale Break taps.
- Scale Break has a boss shield-health target, so under-geared players need more
  accurate and consistent tapping while correctly geared players finish sooner.
- Use defensive stats for a bounded one-time ember shield or Ward contribution,
  if included in tuning, but never allow unlimited wave collisions.
- Ember pickups remain skill-based and are not generated by equipment.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Lane collision and movement tests.
- Scale Break timer and tap-count tests.
- Gear responsiveness and shield caps.
- Regression test proving route completion still requires avoiding or surviving
  hazards.

## Stage 5 — Bullet hell and the final gauntlet

### Boss 9: Xar'Mal, the Lich Emperor

**Mechanic:** Soul Dodge. Survive 60 seconds in a square arena against spirals,
falling rain, sweeping lasers, and orbiting rings.

**Skill implementation:**

- Use a five-pixel logical player-heart hitbox.
- Support mouse/touch drag, arrows, WASD, and Shift slow movement.
- Load a deterministic pattern timeline.
- Implement telegraphs, projectiles, lasers, rings, and collision checks.
- On hit, remove one heart and grant one second of invincibility.
- Clear after 60 seconds.
- Award 3★ with no more than one hit.

**Gear scaling:**

- Use Speed to alter movement acceleration and deceleration within a safe range.
- Use Defense to reduce damage from an unavoidable collision only if the
  encounter's data explicitly permits partial mitigation; the default hit still
  removes a heart.
- Use Ward for extra hearts, capped by the global limit.
- Use Attack and Pierce during short vulnerability windows in the pattern,
  allowing successful movement plus correct attack timing to reduce boss
  stability.
- Vulnerability windows must not be required for basic survival, but insufficient
  damage should prevent a clear after the survival timer.
- Gear cannot enlarge the heart hitbox or grant permanent invincibility.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Pattern timeline determinism.
- Projectile and laser collision tests.
- Invincibility timing tests.
- Movement-stat bounds tests.
- Stability damage tests for vulnerability windows.

### Boss 10: The Hollow King

**Mechanic:** The Five Trials. A shared-heart gauntlet combining Burst, Rhythm,
Parry, Soul Dodge, and Crown Strike.

**Skill implementation:**

- Build a phase controller with shared hearts.
- Phase 1: six-second CPS Burst, target at least 8 CPS.
- Phase 2: 30-second four-lane Rhythm using Morwenna's Groove rules.
- Restore one heart between phases 2 and 3.
- Phase 3: 12 Parry attacks with Aldric-style feints.
- Phase 4: 40-second Soul Dodge pattern remix using Xar'Mal's rules.
- Phase 5: three Crown Strike timing hits with a 5% zone and fast needle.
- Clear only after all phases finish.
- Award 2★ with at most two hearts lost and 3★ with zero hearts lost.

**Gear scaling:**

- Give the Hollow King a per-phase health/stability target rather than one
  undifferentiated health number.
- Phase 1 uses Attack to increase burst damage, but still requires active taps.
- Phase 2 uses Attack to increase successful-note progress and Defense to provide
  a small Groove-loss mitigation, never below a meaningful minimum.
- Phase 3 uses Attack/Pierce for successful parry counter damage; failed parries
  still remove hearts.
- Phase 4 uses Speed, Ward, and bounded Defense effects while preserving the
  bullet patterns and collision rules.
- Phase 5 uses Attack/Pierce for successful Crown Strike damage, while Focus
  affects the tiny timing zone.
- Recommended tier-10 gear should make the combined stability targets practical,
  not automatic.
- Under-geared players may still clear through excellent execution if they meet
  every phase's minimum, but they should have less tolerance for missed damage
  opportunities.

**Tests and tuning:**

- Phase ordering and transition tests.
- Shared-heart accounting tests.
- Exactly-one-heart restoration test after Phase 2.
- Cross-boss mechanic reuse tests.
- Stability accumulation tests across all five phases.
- Regression tests proving Give Up, pause, or closing the fight awards no
  partial clear or reward.

## Reward and progression integration

After a completed first clear:

- Grant the boss's gold and XP.
- Grant the guaranteed Legendary.
- Record the clear and best stars.
- Grant one-time star rewards only when that star level is newly achieved.

For repeat clears:

- Grant reduced gold and trophies.
- Give a 5% Boss Essence chance on repeat 3★ clears.
- Never duplicate the guaranteed first-clear Legendary.

Gear scaling must not bypass campaign unlock requirements. A player still needs
the previous boss clear and the minimum level for the next boss, even if their
equipment is unusually strong.

## Validation strategy

Each development stage should be completed in this order:

1. Implement and test the shared state machine in `core`.
2. Add deterministic tests for timing, input judgment, randomness, damage,
   stability, hearts, and stars.
3. Add data files for boss values, charts, and pattern scripts.
4. Connect the graphical client with keyboard and mouse/touch controls.
5. Test under-geared, recommended-gear, and over-geared profiles.
6. Verify that:
   - Zero-input runs cannot clear.
   - Perfect skill with weak gear remains possible but harder.
   - Appropriate gear improves progress or tolerance.
   - Gear never converts an invalid input into a valid one.
   - 3★ conditions remain skill-based.
   - Rewards are granted exactly once for first clears and star upgrades.
   - Save data remains correct after defeat, retry, pause, Give Up, and restart.
